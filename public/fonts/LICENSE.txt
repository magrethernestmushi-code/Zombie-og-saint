caption-font.ttf is DejaVu Sans Bold, from the DejaVu Fonts project
(https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/), used here for burned-in text/caption
rendering in exported video.

permissive license (Bitstream Vera License + public-domain additions)
that allows free use, modification and redistribution, including in
